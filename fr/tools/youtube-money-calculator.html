Understanding YouTube RPM vs CPM in 2026

CPM (Cost Per Mille) is what advertisers pay YouTube per 1,000 ad impressions, whereas RPM (Revenue Per Mille) is your actual take-home earnings per 1,000 total video views after YouTube's 45% revenue share. Choosing a high-RPM niche like Finance, Software Tutorials, or B2B Reviews allows creators to earn 5x to 10x more with the exact same view count.

Questions Fréquentes (FAQ)

How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views (RPM) in 2026?

Finance and SaaS channels earn $12–$35 per 1,000 views, Tech channels earn $6–$15, and Gaming/Entertainment channels earn $1.50–$4.00 per 1,000 views.
