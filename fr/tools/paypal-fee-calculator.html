About the PayPal & Stripe Fee Calculator

Freelancers, agencies, and e-commerce sellers lose thousands of dollars every year to payment gateway processing fees. Whether you use PayPal Invoicing (3.49% + $0.49) or Stripe Checkout (2.9% + $0.30), simply adding 3.49% to your invoice is mathematically incorrect because the processor also charges a fee on the added fee itself.

Our reverse fee calculator uses the exact gross-up formula Gross = (Net + Fixed Fee) / (1 - Fee%) so you know down to the cent how much to invoice your client to walk away with 100% of your target payout.

Questions Fréquentes (FAQ)

How much does PayPal charge for Goods and Services in 2026?

For standard US domestic commercial transactions, PayPal charges 3.49% + $0.49 per invoice/checkout transaction, or 2.99% + $0.49 for standard credit/debit card payments. International transactions add a 1.50% cross-border surcharge.

How do I calculate how much to ask for so I receive the exact amount after PayPal fees?

Use the reverse gross-up formula: Ask Amount = (Target Net Amount + Fixed Fee) / (1 - Percentage Fee). Look at the blue "Ask Client For (Reverse)" card above for the exact figure.
