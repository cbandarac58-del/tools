How to Compress a PDF Under 100KB or 200KB Without Losing Quality

Updated: November 2026 · 5 min read · SmartToolzAI Editorial

Government visa portals, university admissions systems, and corporate job boards frequently enforce strict 100KB or 200KB PDF upload limits. Yet a single scanned passport page or resume exported from Word can easily exceed 2MB.

Why Are PDF Files So Large?

  • Uncompressed Object Streams: Standard PDF exports store metadata, font tables, and vector paths without stream compression.
  • Embedded Full-Set Fonts: Including entire font families adds 300KB-800KB even for a 1-page document.
  • High-DPI Scanned Images: Scanning documents at 600 DPI instead of 150 DPI increases file size by 16x.

3 Steps to Get Any PDF Under 100KB Safely

First, avoid uploading sensitive passports, bank statements, or tax forms to cloud PDF compressors that store your files on remote servers. Instead, use a 100% client-side browser PDF compressor that strips redundant metadata and compresses object streams directly on your device.

Recommended Settings for <100KB Portals:
1. Resolution: 144 DPI (ideal for screen reading)
2. Color Mode: Grayscale for text/ID documents
3. Stream Compression: Enabled (Object Streams ON)
