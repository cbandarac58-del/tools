ChatGPT & Claude Prompt Engineering Cheat Sheet (CO-STAR Framework)

Updated: November 2026 · 5 min read · SmartToolzAI Editorial

The difference between a mediocre AI response and an expert-grade deliverable comes down to how you structure your prompt. The CO-STAR Framework gives LLMs like GPT-4o and Claude 3.7 Sonnet the exact context they need.

The 6 Pillars of the CO-STAR Framework

C - Context: Background information on the user and project
O - Objective: The exact task you want the AI to accomplish
S - Style: Writing or coding style (e.g., McKinsey consultant, clean TypeScript)
T - Tone: Emotional or professional attitude (e.g., authoritative, direct)
A - Audience: Who will read or use the output
R - Response Format: Markdown table, JSON, step-by-step checklist

Why One-Line Prompts Fail

When you type "Write an email to a client," the model guesses your industry, relationship, and goal. When you specify role, constraints, and output structure, the model narrows its probability distribution to high-value professional patterns.
