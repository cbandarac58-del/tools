The Reverse Fee Formula: How to Receive 100% of Your Invoice

Many freelancers make the mistake of adding 3.49% to a $1,000 invoice ($1,034.90). However, PayPal charges 3.49% on the total amount ($1,034.90), leaving you short! To receive the exact net amount, use the gross-up formula:

Ask Amount = (Target Net Amount + Fixed Fee) / (1 - Percentage Fee)

Example ($1,000 via PayPal 3.49% + $0.49):
($1,000 + $0.49) / (1 - 0.0349) = $1,036.67
